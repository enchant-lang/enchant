The common bundle has separate proof, premise feasibility, logical conclusion
and consumer-policy judgments. Version 2 adds a closed feasibility candidate
slot. Version 1 packets retain their closed schema and receive fresh version 2
checking judgments when reopened. A historical producer's results are never an
input. Proof and feasibility allowances are separate prospective reservations.

Installed choices are finite Boolean/word exhaustion and graph-based linear
integer refutation. Finite premise search establishes either a freshly replayed
witness or a checked contradiction. Integer premises accept explicit point
coordinates and/or a contradiction certificate bound to the deterministically
derived background. Integers are not enumerated. Unsupported strategies refuse.

Missing vacuity policy defaults to report. Explicit nil, strings, booleans and
unknown policies refuse in constructors, ordinary validators, graph readers and
request headers. All readers preserve allow/report/reject. Request headers now
use the qualified one-pass algorithm; this is separate from typed admission.

For checked conditional entailment, witnessed premises accept all policies.
Inconsistent premises accept/report/reject according to allow/report/reject.
Unestablished premises accept/report/leave incomplete. A rejected proof cannot
be repaired by the vacuity policy. Conflicting independent evidence refuses an
obligation judgment. Native-only UNSAT remains a solver claim.
