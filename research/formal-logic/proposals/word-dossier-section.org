** A byte can carry its meaning across both solvers
A counter that fits in eight bits wraps from =255= to =0=. That is a different promise from unbounded integer addition. Beam Lisp can now keep the width, the operations, the remembered facts and the checking rules together in one portable question.

The [[#cell-story-word-request][executed byte example]] asks both Z3 and OxiZ about that wrap. BL then checks the returned point using its own word operations. Change the saved counter to =0= and the same reader rejects it: the solver's status cannot make a wrong point true.

#+begin_rosetta :title "One request, several ways to answer"
Route | What crosses the boundary | What BL checks
Existing native owner | Complete graph question and explicit word policy | Every retained fact and demanded word operation.
Fresh native owner | The same portable request | The same point relation under the same policy.
Supplied point handler | An eight-bit coordinate, as ordinary BL data | Width, value range, resources and the actual proposition.
Saved record reader | A graph packet or a ledger ID | Fresh point replay, without asking a solver.
#+end_rosetta

#+begin_graph :title "Width belongs to the question"
node terms | Word terms | Width and operations | 0,0 | you
node policy | Checking policy | Grammar and budgets | 1,1 | station
node request | Complete request | Terms and assumptions | 1,0 | door
node engines | Z3 or OxiZ | Candidate coordinates | 2,0 | builder
node replay | Fresh BL replay | Check the actual point | 3,0 | you
edge terms -> request | describe | solid
edge policy -> request | bound | solid
edge request -> engines | ask | solid
edge engines -> replay | inspect | solid
#+end_graph

The resource rules apply before expensive word operations. Admission checks even an unused declaration's width and reserves literal work before constructing terms. Point replay charges the coordinates it actually needs and the operations it demands. Raising a work allowance cannot enlarge the language's fixed width grammar.

#+begin_sequence :title "Check the budget before making the value"
lanes graph · original question | admission · BL policy | owner · chosen engine | reader · fresh replay
graph -> admission : Inspect sorts and reserve all literal work.
admission -> owner : Only an admitted complete question reaches native execution.
owner --> reader : Return a status and any word coordinates.
reader -> reader : Reserve demanded work before checking values and operations.
note admission, reader : A budget refusal is incomplete checking. It is not a false mathematical claim.
#+end_sequence

Saved answers also have a more direct storage path. Opening one ledger ID validates that row's existing payload and freshly checks its point. The qualification measures one private row-normalizer call per selected lookup; unrelated records are not decoded on that path. This is an access-path measurement, not a general latency benchmark.

#+begin_honest :title "What this adds to a formal language"
This policy gives typed word propositions and independently replayed finite points a common home in BL. A native unsatisfiable answer remains a solver claim. Quantifier replay, further word operators, a checked word proof format, Source-level word outcomes and a compiler preservation theorem need their own implementations and evidence. The separate Lean magnitude model proves arithmetic bounds; it does not prove this evaluator or either solver correct.
#+end_honest
